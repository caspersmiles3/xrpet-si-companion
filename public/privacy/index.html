XRPet SI Companion Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

XRPet stores companion preferences, memories, XP, rooms, notification history, and watched public XRPL addresses primarily in your browser's local storage.

XRPet does not request or store seed phrases or private keys. Wallet connections are intended to expose only public account information required for the requested features.

Live market, XRPL, Ripple/XRPL update, push-notification, and SI features may use third-party services to process requests. Those services may receive technical request data required to operate their APIs.

For privacy questions, contact shortguy_15@msn.com.
