MODULE 01

XRP Basics

XRP is the native digital asset of the XRP Ledger. It is not a share of Ripple, and owning XRP does not represent ownership in Ripple the company.

NATIVE ASSETXRP

XRP exists directly on the XRP Ledger and is used by the network for transaction fees and account reserve requirements.

NO MININGConsensus network

The XRP Ledger does not rely on proof-of-work mining to validate transactions.

TRANSFERMoves on XRPL

XRP can be sent between XRP Ledger accounts and can also be traded through XRPL's built-in decentralized exchange functionality.

MODULE 02

Ripple is not XRP

Ripple is a technology company. XRP is a digital asset. The XRP Ledger is an open-source public blockchain network. They are related historically and commercially, but they are not the same thing.

RIPPLECompany

Builds enterprise blockchain and digital-asset infrastructure and develops products that can interact with blockchain networks.

XRPDigital asset

The native asset of the XRP Ledger. It can exist and move independently of Ripple.

XRPLPublic network

The distributed ledger where XRP and other supported assets can be transacted.

MODULE 03

XRP Ledger Fundamentals

The XRP Ledger is designed for fast settlement, asset exchange, token issuance, and programmable financial functionality without proof-of-work mining.

CONSENSUSValidator agreement

Independent validators participate in reaching agreement on the state of the ledger.

ISSUED ASSETSTrust lines

XRPL accounts can hold issued currencies through trust lines, which define relationships between an account and an issuer.

BUILT-IN MARKETDEX + AMM

XRPL includes native exchange functionality, including an order-book decentralized exchange and automated market maker features.

MODULE 04

Payments + Settlement

XRPL is built around transferring value and settling transactions on a shared ledger. Different assets can be represented and exchanged on the network, while XRP can function as a native bridge asset in some payment paths.

PAYMENTSAccount to account

XRPL Payment transactions can move XRP or supported issued assets between accounts.

DESTINATION TAGRecipient routing

Services such as exchanges may use destination tags to identify a specific customer or internal recipient.

PATHSAsset conversion

XRPL payment paths can use available liquidity to convert between compatible assets during settlement.
